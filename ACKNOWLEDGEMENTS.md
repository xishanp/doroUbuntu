# Acknowledgements / 致谢

## iPupil

LinuxHub 的基础框架来源于 UP 主 **iPupil**。

LinuxHub 在该基础上继续整合和开发了 Android 应用层、Ubuntu 部署、KDE Plasma 6、Anland 显示链路、GPU 后端策略、输入法桥接、触控交互、音频和终端体验。

感谢 iPupil 分享原始框架与技术思路。

The foundational framework used by LinuxHub originates from content creator **iPupil**.

LinuxHub extends that foundation with Android integration, Ubuntu deployment, KDE Plasma 6, the Anland display pipeline, GPU backend policies, IME bridging, touch interaction, audio, and terminal integration.

Special thanks to iPupil for sharing the original framework and technical work.
